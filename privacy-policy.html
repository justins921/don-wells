Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Don Wells Custom Home Improvements LLC, doing business as Don Wells Lawn & Snow (“we”, “us”, or “our”), operates the website at donwellslawnandsnow.com (the “Service”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Service, how we use it, and the choices you have. We respect your privacy: we do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. When you submit our “Get a Free Estimate” contact form, we collect the details you provide so we can respond to your request:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Property address
  • The message and any details you include about the work you need

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect payment information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to:

  • Respond to your estimate request or inquiry;
  • Schedule, provide, and follow up on the lawn care and snow removal services you ask about; and
  • Keep a record of our communications with you.

We will not use your information for any unrelated purpose without your consent.

Cookies and Analytics

This website is a simple, static site. It does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use third-party analytics or marketing trackers. Because of this, you will not see a cookie consent banner—there is nothing to opt into. Your browser may still store standard technical data needed to load the page, which is not used to identify you.

Server Logs

Like most websites, our hosting and email providers automatically record basic technical information for security and reliability—such as your IP address, browser type, and the date and time of a request. This data is used only to operate and protect the Service and is not used to build a profile of you.

Service Providers

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to operate the Service. Our website is hosted on Vercel, and contact form submissions are delivered to us by email through Resend. These providers process your information only to deliver their service to us and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We encourage you to review their privacy practices.

Data Retention

We keep the information you submit through the contact form for as long as needed to respond to your request and to maintain a reasonable record of our business communications. You may ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact details below.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or opt out of certain uses. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in the “Contact Us” section. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Security

The security of your information matters to us, and we use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect it. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to Other Sites

Our Service may contain links to websites we do not operate. If you click a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, the content or privacy practices of any third-party sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

Our Service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Compliance With Laws

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and post the new version here. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Changes are effective when posted.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, please contact us at info@donwellslawnandsnow.com or 920-960-5547.
